# Gallery content policy — v4 (approved in principle 2026-11-12; the nuance gets taught later)

**Status:** draft, 2026-11-12. Nothing here is live yet. Decisions you made are marked **✅ DECIDED**. Calls I made for you, based on your goals and what similar sites do, are marked **☑ PROPOSED** (tell me if you disagree). Once you approve it, this becomes the AI scanner's rulebook and the moderation team's guide.

**Built from:** your Terms of Service and your answers, plus the published community rules of Meta / Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit and Discord, and the art platforms closest to Gallery (DeviantArt, ArtStation, Pixiv). Where those platforms agree, the rule is here. Where Gallery is different (it's an art site, so fiction gets more room), that's noted.

**Covers everything social on Gallery:** posts (image + title + caption + hashtags), stories, comments, direct messages, commission requests and chats, shop listings, profile pictures, banners, bios, usernames and display names. The question is always **"should this be on Gallery at all?"**, not just "is this outfit OK?".

**Rule codes:** every rule has a short code (for example `SEX-2`). The AI must name the code(s) when it flags something, so the team can track flags by rule, by person and over time, and measure how accurate the AI is per rule.

## The baseline ✅ DECIDED

**If it would normally be allowed on Instagram, it's allowed on Gallery** (except where this rulebook is deliberately stricter, for example no nudity). The rules below set the outline; the team teaches the AI the finer nuance over time through its yes/no answers and lessons, not by making this document longer.

## How to judge anything: five checks

1. **What is shown or said?** Look at the image *and* all the text together. A harmless picture with a hateful caption is a hateful post.
2. **Depicted or promoted?** Showing something in a story (a drug deal in a crime comic) is very different from promoting, selling, praising or teaching it. Gallery allows depiction far more than promotion.
3. **Real or fictional?** Real, identifiable people and real photos of real events get a much stricter standard than drawn or invented characters.
4. **Who is it aimed at?** Content targeting a specific real person or a protected group is treated more seriously than general content.
5. **Where does it appear?** Profile pictures, banners, usernames, display names and shop thumbnails appear everywhere, so they get the strictest standard. Private messages get the same rules but more room for normal conversation between friends.

## Severity levels (what the AI attaches to every flag)

| Level | Meaning | Team priority |
|---|---|---|
| **ZERO** | Zero tolerance: child safety, terrorism, real-person sexual abuse, credible threats | Look immediately |
| **URGENT** | Someone may be in danger (self-harm intent, a threat, a minor at risk) | Look immediately, for safety not punishment |
| **SEVERE** | Clearly against the rules | Same day |
| **REVIEW** | Probably against the rules, or a grey area | When possible |
| **CHECK** | Probably fine, but the AI isn't sure | Batch review; this is mainly for teaching the AI |

While the AI is learning, it should **flag when unsure** (CHECK) rather than pass. Nothing is removed automatically; the team decides.

## CHILD — Children and minors (ZERO TOLERANCE)

- `CHILD-1` **Sexual content involving minors:** any sexual, sexualised or suggestive depiction of a minor, drawn or real, any art style. "She's really 1,000 years old" and similar excuses don't change it. Based on how they look (body proportions and face together), not height alone.
- `CHILD-2` **Abuse of minors:** gore, torture or physical or sexual abuse aimed at a child.
- `CHILD-3` **Grooming:** an adult seeking private contact with a minor, asking for photos, age, location or secrets, or sexual talk with a minor.
- `CHILD-4` **Under-13 users:** someone saying they are under 13 (Gallery's minimum age). This is URGENT, not a punishment.
- `CHILD-5` **Exploiting minors:** sharing real photos of real children in a sexualised or mocking way, or exposing a minor's personal information.
- **Allowed:** children in ordinary, non-sexual scenes (family, school, play, sad or dramatic stories without abuse).

## SEX — Nudity and sexual content

- ✅ **DECIDED:** no actual nudity, for now. Revealing or provocative outfits and alluring poses **are allowed**. Implied nudity **is allowed** when the person is properly covered.
- `SEX-1` **Nudity:** visible nipples, genitals or bare buttocks, including artistic, classical and figure-study nudes, and see-through clothing that shows these.
- `SEX-2` **Sexual acts:** explicit or clearly simulated sex acts, sexual touching, or close-ups made to be sexual.
- `SEX-3` **Pornographic or fetish content:** content made to arouse, such as fetish material or bodily fluids in a sexual context.
- `SEX-4` **Sexual services and solicitation:** offering or asking for sexual services, "spicy" content, OnlyFans or links to adult sites, or sexual requests in comments or messages.
- `SEX-5` **Sexual harassment:** unwanted sexual comments or messages aimed at a person.
- **Grey area (REVIEW):** lingerie, and implied nudity where the covering is thin, partial or barely there.
- **Allowed:** fashion, swimwear, revealing outfits, alluring poses, kissing and romance.

## VIOL — Violence, gore and shocking content

- `VIOL-1` **Real gore:** real photos or video of real injuries, deaths, accidents, attacks or bodies.
- `VIOL-2` **Glorifying real violence:** celebrating or praising real attacks, mass shootings, their perpetrators or victims' suffering.
- `VIOL-3` **Gratuitous torture:** torture or extreme gore shown purely for shock, with no story or artistic purpose.
- `VIOL-4` **Violence against a real or protected target:** art showing violence against a specific real person, or against people *because of* their religion, race or other protected trait (also see `HATE-3`).
- **Grey area (REVIEW):** heavy fictional gore, dismemberment, and body horror.
- ☑ **PROPOSED:** realistic *staged* violence without gore (arguments, fights, someone being cornered) is **allowed**. It's only flagged when it targets a real person or a protected group (`VIOL-4`, `HATE-3`).
- **Allowed:** action, fights, battles, monsters, horror, and blood or injuries in a clear story or fictional context.

## HARM — Self-harm, suicide and eating disorders

- `HARM-1` **Glorifying or instructional self-harm:** showing self-harm as attractive, or explaining methods.
- `HARM-2` **Encouraging self-harm:** telling someone to hurt or kill themselves (including "kys"), suicide challenges, or suicide pacts.
- `HARM-3` **Pro-eating-disorder:** "thinspo", or promoting starving or purging.
- `HARM-4` **Person at risk (URGENT):** someone saying *they* want to hurt or kill themselves. The team reaches out with help; this is never a punishment.
- ☑ **PROPOSED:** all URGENT and ZERO flags alert the team straight away (a badge in admin plus an email), instead of waiting in the queue.
- **Allowed:** sad, dark or emotional art, recovery stories, and healed scars.

## HATE — Hate speech and hateful conduct

Protected traits: race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, serious illness, age, and immigration status.

- `HATE-1` **Slurs and dehumanising language** about a protected group, including misspelled, censored or emoji versions, and words written inside images.
- `HATE-2` **Hateful stereotypes and mockery** that attack people for a protected trait.
- `HATE-3` **Hateful imagery:** art that mocks, threatens or celebrates harm to a protected group, such as a scene built to glorify attacking someone for their religion.
- `HATE-4` **Hate symbols and hate groups:** swastikas, Nazi or white-supremacist symbols and codes (for example "14/88"), or praising hate groups.
- **Allowed:** calm opinions, beliefs and religious views; respectful debate; art that *shows* injustice in order to criticise it.
- ☑ **PROPOSED:** hate symbols in a clear historical, educational or critical context (for example a WWII scene) are **allowed**, but always flagged as CHECK so the team can confirm the context. Using them approvingly is `HATE-4`.

## BULLY — Harassment and bullying

- `BULLY-1` **Targeted insults and abuse** aimed at a person, including swearing *at* someone.
- `BULLY-2` **Threats and intimidation:** "I know where you live", threats to someone's account or livelihood, or blackmail.
- `BULLY-3` **Unwanted repeated contact:** messaging someone who has said stop, or creating new accounts to keep contacting them.
- `BULLY-4` **Pile-ons and brigading:** encouraging others to attack, report or harass someone.
- `BULLY-5` **Degrading content about real people:** humiliating edits or art of a real private person, or body-shaming someone.
- **Allowed:** harsh but constructive art critique ("this doesn't work, here's why"), disagreement, and satire of public figures.

## THREAT — Threats and violent extremism (ZERO TOLERANCE)

- `THREAT-1` **Credible threats** of violence against a real person or place, including "jokes" that read as real.
- `THREAT-2` **Terrorism and violent extremism:** praising, supporting or recruiting for terrorist or violent extremist groups, sharing their propaganda, or celebrating attacks.
- `THREAT-3` **Planning or encouraging an attack,** or making weapons for violence.
- **Allowed:** weapons in fantasy, action, war and historical art; and fictional villains.

## ILLEGAL — Drugs, weapons and regulated goods

- `ILLEGAL-1` **Selling or buying drugs** (including in posts, messages and shop listings), or arranging drug deals.
- `ILLEGAL-2` **Selling weapons**, ammunition, explosives or parts.
- `ILLEGAL-3` **Other illegal or regulated sales:** prescription drugs, alcohol, tobacco or vape sales, counterfeit goods, stolen goods, fake IDs, or human or animal trafficking.
- `ILLEGAL-4` **How-to instructions** for making drugs, weapons or explosives, or for committing crimes.
- `ILLEGAL-5` **Glamorising drug use:** content that presents drug use as cool or aspirational, encourages it, or shows how to get drugs.
- ☑ **PROPOSED:** drug use shown as part of a story (a nightclub scene or a crime comic) is **allowed**. It's flagged as REVIEW only when it's glamorised or promotional. Selling is never allowed (`ILLEGAL-1`).
- **Allowed:** stories that *show* crime or drug use (noir, crime comics, gangster art), and alcohol or smoking as part of a scene.

## REAL — Real people, privacy and consent

- `REAL-1` **Sexual or nude images of real people:** including deepfakes and AI images of celebrities or private people. This is ZERO TOLERANCE.
- `REAL-2` **Intimate images shared without consent:** "revenge porn" and sextortion. ZERO TOLERANCE.
- `REAL-3` **Doxxing:** home addresses, phone numbers, private emails, ID documents, workplaces of private people, or screenshots of private conversations shared to expose someone.
- `REAL-4` **Impersonation:** pretending to be another artist, a real person, Gallery staff or a brand.
- `REAL-5` **Degrading edits of private people** (also see `BULLY-5`).
- **Allowed:** respectful fan art and portraits of public figures, satire and caricature of public figures (not sexual), and sharing your *own* contact details for business.

## SCAM — Fraud, scams and spam

- `SCAM-1` **Off-platform payment:** asking to be paid outside Gallery for commissions or shop items (PayPal, CashApp, Venmo, crypto, gift cards). This is already a strike in your rules.
- `SCAM-2` **Scams and phishing:** fake giveaways, "you won", fake support accounts, malware links, and login or verification code requests.
- `SCAM-3` **Fake commissions and bait-and-switch:** taking payment without intending to deliver, or listings that misrepresent what's sold.
- `SCAM-4` **Spam:** repetitive self-promotion, copy-pasted comments, link-only comments, bot activity, "follow for follow", or buying or selling likes and followers.
- `SCAM-5` **Get-rich-quick, crypto and investment schemes,** and "sugar daddy" offers.

## LANG — Bad language

- `LANG-1` **Slurs** (also see `HATE-1`).
- `LANG-2` **Swearing aimed at a person** as an insult (also see `BULLY-1`).
- `LANG-3` **Graphic sexual language** aimed at someone, or explicit sexual writing in public posts.
- `LANG-4` **Profane usernames, display names or bios:** ☑ **PROPOSED:** no swearing at all in usernames, display names and bios, because they show everywhere.
- ☑ **PROPOSED, allowed:** casual swearing that isn't aimed at anyone, in posts, comments and messages ("this is f***ing amazing").
- **Applies to everything:** words inside images, misspellings, leetspeak, censoring ("f*ck"), emoji codes, and any language, not just English.

## AIART — AI art and copying

- `AIART-1` **Unlabelled AI art:** ✅ **DECIDED, track and notify.** The AI looks for signs that unlabelled art was AI-made. When it suspects AI:
  1. The post is **tracked** (logged against the post and the artist), never hidden or removed.
  2. The artist gets an **automated notice**: *"We've detected possible AI use in your post. Please label it as AI-assisted, or send us evidence that it isn't (for example work-in-progress shots or the layered file)."*
  3. The team can see every tracked post, whether the artist labelled it or sent evidence, and repeat cases per artist.
  4. Strikes are only ever given by a person, not by the notice.
- `AIART-2` **AI style theft:** AI work clearly made to copy a specific living artist without consent (already in your Terms).
- `AIART-3` **Reposting or tracing someone else's art as your own,** or removing watermarks or signatures.
- **Allowed:** labelled AI art; fan art (your Terms); and studies or references that credit the original.

## ANIMAL — Animals

- `ANIMAL-1` **Real animal cruelty,** or real animals being hurt for entertainment.
- `ANIMAL-2` **Any sexual content involving animals.** ZERO TOLERANCE.
- `ANIMAL-3` **Selling protected or endangered animals** or animal parts.
- **Allowed:** animals in any normal, fantasy or story context, and hunting or food scenes as part of a story.

## DANGER — Dangerous acts and misinformation

- `DANGER-1` **Dangerous challenges or stunts** likely to cause serious injury, especially if minors could copy them.
- `DANGER-2` **Harmful health misinformation:** for example, telling people to drink bleach or to stop prescribed treatment.
- *(Lighter touch than general social media: Gallery is an art site, so political opinions in art are allowed. Only clearly harmful false claims are flagged.)*

## ACCOUNT — Account-level behaviour (tracked across posts, not one item)

The AI can't see these in one post. The system should track them per person over time:

- `ACCOUNT-1` **Ban evasion:** new accounts that look like a banned user (your rules already handle the same email; this would add same-device and same-pattern signals).
- `ACCOUNT-2` **Repeat offender:** the same rule flagged several times for one person within 30 days.
- `ACCOUNT-3` **Spam bursts:** the same message to many people, or many comments in a short time.
- `ACCOUNT-4` **Fake engagement:** groups of accounts liking or following each other to game Trending and Rising Stars.

## What the AI must output for every scan

For every scan, the AI reports:
- **Rule codes:** the codes that apply (for example `ILLEGAL-5`, `VIOL-4`), or `NONE`.
- **Severity:** ZERO, URGENT, SEVERE, REVIEW, CHECK, or none.
- **What it saw:** in plain words, covering the image *and* the text.
- **Why:** which rule applies and which part of the content matches it.
- **Confidence:** low, medium or high.

Everything is logged, so the admin can show, for example, "most-flagged rules this week", "this person's flags by rule", and "how often the AI was right about `HATE-3`".

- **Terms of Service:** I'll update your Terms to match how moderation really works. Right now they say AI-flagged content is hidden and auto-removed after 14 days. The new text will say the AI flags content, a person reviews it, and only a person removes anything, and the content rules will match this rulebook.
