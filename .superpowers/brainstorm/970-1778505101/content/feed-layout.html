What does the main feed show?

Stories at the top like Instagram — the question is what's in the scroll below

A

Following Only

The feed shows posts exclusively from artists you follow. Clean and personal — you control exactly what you see.

Pros

  • Predictable, curated experience
  • Users feel in control

Cons

  • New users with no follows see nothing
  • Harder for new artists to get discovered
B

Following + Recommended (Two Tabs)

Two tabs at the top of the feed — Following for people you follow, For You for Gallery's personalised recommendations. Like TikTok / Instagram Reels.

Pros

  • New users get content immediately
  • Artists can reach people who don't follow them
  • Powers the Rising Stars discovery

Cons

  • Slightly more complex
  • Recommended tab needs the algorithm to work well
