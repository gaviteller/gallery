How do buyers browse the shop?

Two approaches to how buyers discover art for sale on Gallery

A

Global Shop Feed

There's a dedicated Shop tab on Gallery where buyers can browse all artwork listed for sale across the entire platform — filtered by style, medium, price, and type. Like Etsy or Gumroad. Discovery-first.

Pros

  • Buyers can discover new artists while shopping
  • Bigger surface area for sales

Cons

  • More complex to build
  • Smaller artists can get buried
B

Artist Profile Only

Shop listings live on the artist's profile page only. Buyers land on an artist they like and can browse their shop from there. No separate global shop tab.

Pros

  • Simpler to build
  • Keeps focus on the artist relationship

Cons

  • Buyers must already know whose shop to visit
  • Harder to stumble onto new artists
C

Both — Profile + Global Browse

Shop listings live on the artist's profile and a global Shop tab lets buyers browse everything. Best of both worlds — but more to build.

Pros

  • Maximum discovery surface
  • Artists benefit from both channels

Cons

  • Most work to build
  • Can dilute focus if not curated well
